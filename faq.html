OpenMOVR and MDA

What is OpenMOVR?

OpenMOVR is the open source program of the Muscular Dystrophy Association's MOVR registry. It publishes registry figures anyone can cite, the code behind them, and progress on MOVR 2.0 as it happens.

How is OpenMOVR related to MDA's MOVR website?

Both are part of MOVR. MDA MOVR on mda.org is MDA's official home for the registry, and it is where participants will join MOVR 2.0. OpenMOVR is MOVR's open source program. It shares the tools, figures, methods and code that anyone can use and improve.

Until MDA's MOVR 2.0 site launches, MOVR updates appear here first. Everything on OpenMOVR stays open after that.

What is OpenMOVR for?

MOVR is a nonprofit registry, stewarded by MDA alongside patient advocates. OpenMOVR builds open source tools for it and gives it technical support.

It is also a goodwill space. Open source has a long history of volunteers improving tools that small teams can't fund alone. Google Summer of Code contributors, technologists, data scientists, and PhD and graduate students help make MOVR data more useful and analytics more possible, especially when funding is tight. In return they get real clinical data problems, mentorship and portfolio work. Why contribute.

Where can I see where MOVR stands right now?

The MOVR progress page shows MOVR 2.0 milestones, pilot results and MOVR Data Hub figures on one page, each with its population and source. It's the page to share.

Data and open science

Will MOVR 2.0 be open source?

MOVR data is built for the whole neuromuscular research community, and that is what makes open source possible. The OpenMOVR App, the analytics library and this site are all open source, and the MOVR front door is being built here too.

The data itself is shared carefully. Academic researchers can use it for free, under a strict data use agreement aligned with what participants consented to. The MDA MOVR team monitors every use to make sure it is safe, fair and actually being used. OpenMOVR helps amplify that work, and we keep looking for new ways to share more.

Who can use MOVR data, and what does it cost?

MOVR runs under an IRB-approved research protocol, and every use of its data has to fit what participants consented to. Academic researchers can use MOVR data for free, under a strict data use agreement.

Requests are reviewed by a governance board and advisory committees. In MOVR 1.0 these groups included patient advocates, clinicians and experts in registry science. MOVR 2.0's governance board and advisory committees are being formed now. Once data is shared, the MDA MOVR team monitors how it is used.

Companies and other organizations can write to mdamovr@mdausa.org to talk about working with MOVR.

How do I request MOVR data?

Submit the MOVR data request form. Legacy data from USNDR (2013–2018) and the MOVR Data Hub (2019–2025) is available for approved research. MOVR 2.0 data requests open once its access rules are set.

How are participants protected?

Only counts are published, and groups too small to be safe are hidden. Record-level data is shared only under a data use agreement, after review, and the MDA MOVR team monitors how it is used.

Can I cite figures from OpenMOVR?

Yes. Use the Community Snapshot in the OpenMOVR App, which gives a suggested citation, or the progress page. Always cite a figure with its population, such as "MOVR Data Hub participants, excluding USNDR legacy participants", along with the app version or date you read it from.

MOVR 2.0

What is MOVR 2.0?

The next generation of MDA's neuromuscular disease registry. Instead of clinics entering data by hand, participants join online, consent, and connect their own health records, and get value back for taking part. More about MOVR 2.0.

When does MOVR 2.0 launch?

Recruitment for the full launch opens in Q1 2027. Email mdamovr@mdausa.org to hear when enrollment opens.

Who can join?

MOVR 2.0 opens to people living with neuromuscular disease and to their parents and caregivers. The disease communities in the first phase will be announced with recruitment.

Where will I sign up?

On MDA's MOVR site at mda.org when recruitment opens. OpenMOVR will link straight to it.

Past participants

I was enrolled in the original MOVR. What happened to my data?

Your participation has concluded, and your consent has been withdrawn as planned. The data you contributed continues to support neuromuscular disease research through approved studies.

Can I get a copy of my MOVR data?

Yes. Request it through the MOVR data request form.

I was in the MOVR 2.0 pilot. What now?

The pilot is closed, and you should have received a thank-you email from us. We'll be in touch when recruitment for MOVR 2.0 opens in 2027. Read the pilot update.

Contributors

How can I contribute?

Read why contribute and the developer docs, pick a repository on GitHub, and open an issue or a small pull request. Data scientists and graduate students are as welcome as engineers.

Does OpenMOVR take part in Google Summer of Code?

Yes. See the GSoC page for project ideas, mentors and how to apply.
