Why open source works here

Small teams, big questions

Open source has a long track record of volunteers improving tools that small teams could never fund on their own. Registries are a good fit for that. The questions are large, the data is valuable, and the people running them are few.

At MOVR, volunteer work turns into cleaner data, better documentation, easier analytics and findings that arrive sooner. It matters most when grants and budgets are tight, because that is when research would otherwise stall.

OpenMOVR is how that work reaches MOVR. It is a goodwill space. Contributors give their time, and patients, families and researchers get the benefit.

Data for contributors

What you can work with

Most contributions need only what is already public. That means published counts, the data dictionary and the code itself.

Record-level data is different. It is available only through MOVR's data request process, under a data use agreement aligned with what participants consented to and reviewed by MOVR's governance board and advisory committees. Contributing code does not grant data access.
