The pilot, 2025 – 2026

What the pilot taught us

Families living with SMA, DMD and LGMD tested competing platforms side by side, and their testing and interviews shaped how MDA chose a technology partner for MOVR 2.0. Here are a few things we now know about this space.

Trust is the foundation

People share their health data because MDA is behind MOVR. They would not give the same trust to an unknown technology company on its own.

Consenting is easy. Connecting records is hard.

Of 122 pilot participants who consented, 44 went on to share health data. That step is what MOVR 2.0 has to make simple.

People stay for what comes back

They join for the mission and stay for the value. That means their own records in one place and a view of what the community's data shows.

What participants told us, and what we're doing about it
