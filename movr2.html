The pilot, 2025 – 2026

What the pilot taught us

Families living with SMA, DMD and LGMD tested competing platforms side by side and told us honestly what earns their trust and what would keep them coming back. Their testing and interviews were a direct part of how MDA chose Vibrent Health as the technology partner for MOVR 2.0. Pilot enrollment is now closed.

The biggest lesson: consenting was not the hard part, connecting health records was. Most people who consented did not get as far as sharing data. Making that step simple is the first thing MOVR 2.0 is built to fix.

What participants told us

Trust in MDA comes first

MDA's history is why people were willing to share. They would not extend the same trust to an unknown technology company on its own.

Explain it simply, and keep explaining

Consent forms get skimmed. Plain language, live Q&A and short reminders over time work better.

Ask before reaching out

Email first, a heads-up before any personal contact, and never a cold call.

Give something back

All their records from every doctor in one place, plain-language summaries, and a look at what the community's data reveals.

What we're carrying forward

Fixing what fell short

Blank home screens, jargon like "EHR" and "encounter", record connections that quietly dropped, and no clear next step after linking records are all on the build list.

Making it effortless

Linking records by typing in a doctor's name stood out. Surveys will show the time up front and say why they matter.

Building for real life

Participants' ideas are on the table: an emergency room summary, guardianship handoffs, and college accommodation letters.

Transparency that keeps going

Broad categories of who uses data and why, with deeper detail for anyone who wants it.

MOVR 2.0 FAQ

About MOVR 2.0

What is MOVR 2.0?

The next generation of MDA's neuromuscular disease registry. Instead of clinics entering data by hand, participants join online, consent, and connect their own health records, and get value back for taking part.

When does MOVR 2.0 launch?

Recruitment for the full launch opens in Q1 2027. Email mdamovr@mdausa.org to hear when enrollment opens.

Who can join?

The first phase opens to people living with SMA, DMD, BMD, LGMD, CMT and ChAT deficiency, and to parents and caregivers. More diseases will follow.

Will MOVR 2.0 be open source?

The tools around it are. OpenMOVR publishes the app, the analytics library and this site as open source, and the MOVR 2.0 front door is being built here in the open.

For previous MOVR participants

I was enrolled in the original MOVR. What happened to my data?

Your participation has concluded, and your consent has been withdrawn as planned. The data you contributed continues to support neuromuscular disease research through approved studies.

I was in the MOVR 2.0 pilot. What now?

The pilot is closed, and you should have received a thank-you email from us. We'll be in touch when recruitment for MOVR 2.0 opens in 2027. Questions? mdamovr@mdausa.org.

For researchers

Can I still access original MOVR data?

Yes. Legacy data from USNDR (2013–2018) and the MOVR Data Hub (2019–2025) remains available for approved research. Submit a data request, or explore study-wide figures in the Community Snapshot.

When can I request MOVR 2.0 data?

MOVR 2.0 data requests open once access rules are set. Until then, write to mdamovr@mdausa.org and ask whether MOVR can answer your question.
